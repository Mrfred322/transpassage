A LITTLE CLARITY

Questions before
you take the leap?

Start here, then ask us about your situation.

I’m not sure which course to choose. Can I enquire?

Yes. Career counselling is a starting point for discussing your interests, strengths and possible education paths. You can enquire even if you haven’t chosen a course.

What should I prepare for the first conversation?

Have a summary of your education, interests and any preferred course or destination. If you already have test results or an application in progress, mention those too.

Can you help with English language tests?

The services listed include IELTS, PTE and TOEFL training and test booking assistance. Contact the team for current availability, schedules and fees.

Do you offer UK visa assistance?

UK visa assistance is listed among Transpassage’s services. Speak to the team about your situation, required documents and the support available. Visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities.

Can I get support after starting my studies abroad?

Transpassage lists support for international students who wish to change university, study course or location, or continue or upgrade their studies.
